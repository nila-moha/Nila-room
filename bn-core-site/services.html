Two pillars, one field: utility-scale BESS

BN CORE GROUP operates on commissioning and operations & maintenance (O&M) projects for battery energy storage systems, through two complementary modes of intervention.

01 — Technical Field Services

On-site intervention on BESS sites

Our technicians intervene directly on site to carry out the technical tasks of a BESS project, in coordination with the EPC, integrator or operator in charge of the project.

Areas of intervention

  • Commissioning: inspection, wiring, functional and performance testing of PCS, BMS and EMS systems.
  • O&M: preventive and corrective maintenance, on-site performance monitoring.
  • Structural repair of BESS containers: welding, corrosion treatment, exterior enclosure restoration.
  • Technical assistance during commissioning and acceptance phases.
  • Application of safety procedures and manufacturer standards in force on site.

Welding & structural repair

Repair of BESS container framing and enclosures: welding, straightening, replacement of damaged panels.

Corrosion protection & sealing

Surface treatment, repainting and weatherproofing of containers exposed to outdoor conditions.

Electrical & mechanical restoration

Diagnosis and repair of auxiliary equipment: cooling, lighting, locking mechanisms and container access.

What we deploy

  • Qualified technicians and engineers for commissioning or O&M missions.
  • Short-term or extended reinforcement, depending on the project's duration and workload.
  • Personnel integrated into the client's organization and supervision on site.
  • Administrative coordination of the deployment (mobilization, logistics, contract follow-up).
02 — Technical Workforce Deployment

Qualified technical personnel, made available to your teams

Rather than a turnkey service, BN CORE GROUP makes qualified technicians and engineers available to reinforce your own teams on a commissioning or O&M project, under your supervision.

Deployment method

A four-step approach

Working version — to be confirmed with BN CORE GROUP before final publication.

Needs qualification

Selection & mobilization

On-site intervention
