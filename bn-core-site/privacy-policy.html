Privacy Policy

How BN CORE GROUP collects and uses personal data submitted through this website.

This Privacy Policy explains how BN CORE GROUP ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data submitted through this website, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

BN CORE GROUP, Ll. Graffplein 15, 1780 Wemmel, Belgium — VAT BE1027.648.484. Contact: info@bncoregroup.com.

2. What data we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide: name, company, email address, phone number, site location, installation type, headcount, project duration, start date, and your message. We do not collect any other personal data through this website.

3. Why we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a commercial proposal. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

4. Who processes it

Your submission is transmitted through Formspree, Inc., a third-party form-processing service, which delivers it to our mailbox. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf; its own privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as necessary to handle your request, and in any case no longer than 3 years after our last contact with you, unless a longer retention period is required by law (for example, accounting obligations linked to a signed contract).

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify or erase your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@bncoregroup.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit — www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be).

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the one in effect.
