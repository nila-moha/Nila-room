Positioning

A specialist partner, not a generalist

BN CORE GROUP is a specialist partner in Technical Field Services for utility-scale battery energy storage system (BESS) projects, on commissioning and operations & maintenance (O&M) missions. We currently operate in the European market.

An extension of our operations toward the Middle East and South Asia is being considered in the medium term — a development in progress, not an already-served market.

Why BN CORE GROUP

An obligation of means, clearly stated

We commit to the quality of the means we deploy — qualified personnel, rigorous method, responsiveness — not to a guaranteed outcome, which by nature is not within our sole control on a shared site.

Methodological rigor

Structured, tracked and documented mobilization and intervention procedures.

Operational responsiveness

A single point of contact and coordinated on-site mobilization.

Compliance & safety

HSE procedures applied on every site of intervention.

Specialized expertise

A team focused exclusively on battery energy storage technologies.
